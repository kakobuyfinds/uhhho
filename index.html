How to order and estimate the landed cost

From product link to warehouse check

  1. Open the CAFANS listing and find the original seller URL. Paste that URL into OrientDig and check that it resolves to the item you saved.
  2. Confirm the color, size and quantity. Compare the seller's measurements with a garment you already own, using the same measurement points.
  3. If warehouse QC photos are available, compare the size tag, color and visible details against your order. Request a close-up if a detail you care about is unclear.
  4. Ask for a parcel quote using your destination and packed dimensions. Check which fees and taxes are already included before you add the quote to your budget.

OrientDig spreadsheet FAQ

What is an OrientDig spreadsheet?

It is a product-link directory for comparing items before using a shopping agent. This page uses CAFANS listings as its catalog source.

Are the product images warehouse QC photos?

The jacket image comes from the CAFANS catalog. For QC, ask your agent for warehouse photos of the specific item and variant you ordered, where available.

Why might my shipping quote differ from the item price?

The listed amount covers the product. International shipping is a separate parcel quote that depends on your destination, route and the carrier's billable weight.

What does the CSV include?

It includes your saved product names, categories, reference prices and CAFANS links. Your shortlist is kept in the current tab until you export it or leave the page.
